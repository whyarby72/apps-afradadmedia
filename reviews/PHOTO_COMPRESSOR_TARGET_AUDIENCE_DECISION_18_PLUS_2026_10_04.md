# Target Audience Decision — 18 and Over

Date: 2026-10-04
Project: Apps AfradadMedia
Product: Photo Compressor: KB Limit

## Canonical decision

`GOOGLE_PLAY_TARGET_AUDIENCE = 18_AND_OVER`

`CHILDREN_INCLUDED_IN_TARGET_AUDIENCE = NO`

## Rationale

Photo Compressor: KB Limit is a general-purpose utility for reducing JPEG/JPG file size toward a user-specified KB/MB limit.

The product is not designed around child-specific content, child-focused education, games, characters, or other child-directed product mechanics.

"Usable by a younger person" is not treated as equivalent to "designed for or targeted to children."

## Effect on current web/privacy candidate

The prior temporary child-inclusive record is superseded.

Privacy wording now states:
- intended audience: adults 18 and over;
- not designed or directed to children.

## Boundary

This is the project decision and repository source of truth.

It does not claim that Google Play Console has already been mutated.

A future change to include minors in the declared target audience requires a fresh compliance review before publication or ad-serving changes.

No Play Console mutation, deployment, merge, Artifact Freeze, or publication is authorized by this decision record.
